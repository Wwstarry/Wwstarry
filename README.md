## Research Interests

My research focuses on **software engineering**, particularly **code generation and code agents**.

I am interested in how large language models:

* reason about complex programming tasks;
* generate human-aligned program logic;
* produce correct and executable behavior in realistic software environments;
* generalize across repositories, tasks, and software development contexts;
* continuously maintain and improve the software they generate.

More broadly, I am drawn to questions about how intelligent systems can achieve deeper forms of **reasoning, generalization, and alignment** through software.


## Research Vision

Rather than pursuing only incremental improvements, I aspire to conduct research that introduces genuinely new paradigms and challenges prevailing assumptions in both academia and industry.

My long-term goal is to contribute work that reshapes our understanding of how intelligent systems **perceive, reason, and interact with the world through software**.
